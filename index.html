Biography

I am currently a Lecturer at the Department of Artificial Intelligence, School of Information and Communication Engineering, Communication University of China, Beijing, China. I use to be a research associate (2012-2014) and assistant professor (2018-2019) at Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Guangdong, China, a visting scholar (2014) at Wayne State University, Michigan, USA, and a post-doctoral researcher (2019-2020) at UT Southwestern Medical Center, Texas, USA.

My research interests focus on artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications in image quality assessment, medical image analysis, biomedical engineering, and multimodal intelligent systems. Recently, I am particularly interested in evaluating the performance of large language models (LLMs) in audio-to-audio translation, and building agents for sciences.

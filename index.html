Biography

I am currently a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, School of Information and Communication Engineering, Communication University of China, Beijing, China. Previously, I worked at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Guangdong, China, as a Research Associate (2012-2014) and as an Assistant Professor (2018-2019). I was also a Visiting Scholar (2014) at Wayne State University, Michigan, USA, and a Postdoctoral Researcher (2019-2020) at UT Southwestern Medical Center, Texas, USA.

My research interests focus on artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications in image quality assessment, medical image analysis, biomedical engineering, and multimodal intelligent systems. Recently, I have become particularly interested in evaluating the performance of large language models (LLMs) for audio-to-audio translation and developing AI agents for scientific research.
